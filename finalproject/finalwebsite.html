Data in Biological Studies

By Zachary Jao

In the field of biological science, data is one of the most crucial aspects of the study.

On the field/in the lab quantitative/qualitative observations are taken into account during research and experiments.

The human mind usually isn't capable of photographic memory, so our observations get jutted down to paper and or digital notes!
-----------------------------------------------------------
- Carl Sagan
Data, in the case of biology, is our way of making sense of/understanding the phenomomena of our natural world. It allows us to see what was once unseen, and make hypotheses and theories afterward. You can't know what food preferences Largemouth Bass have by just looking at them and guessing. You make an experiment and collect data from the results.
In these examples, data is used to show us a record of animal species in the U.S that are endangered and species that are threatened. (Link to data source)
With all of this data and other knowledge passed down from previous researchers, further action can be taken, such as conservation management methods, outreach, education, and government initiative! The story that data tells is the reason why further action in this world can be taken and make Earth a better place for every lifeform.
